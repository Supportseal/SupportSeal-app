# Working the inbox

## The inbox

The **Inbox** lists Conversations from every Product, each marked with its
Product and channel (chat or email). Filter by Product, search by text or ticket
number (`SS-123`), and close a Conversation straight from its row. Unread counts
are per agent and shown in the browser tab title.

## Replying

Open a Conversation and reply. Chat replies reach the visitor live; email
replies are sent from the Product's support sender and thread automatically. A
chat that moves to email stays one Conversation with one ticket number.

Each Conversation is **Open**, **Pending** or **Closed**. A customer reply
reopens a Closed Conversation.

## Notes and tags

Add internal notes (never visible to the customer) and tags to organise work.
Customer details and attachments sit beside the thread.

## Saved replies

Create reusable answers under **Saved replies**, Workspace-wide or scoped to one
Product. In the composer, type `/` or choose **Saved** to search and insert one;
the list is filtered to the Conversation's Product.

## Live and Away

An Admin sets support to **Live** or **Away** in the header.

- **Live:** visitors chat in real time.
- **Away:** the widget shows a message form and asks for an email address; you
  answer by email and the thread stays one Conversation.

## Unanswered-request alerts

If a new Conversation gets no agent reply within its Product's wait time
(default 20 minutes), Admins get one email with the customer's messages and a
link. Adjust or switch it off in the Product's settings. It needs an outbound
mail provider.
