# Getting started

About ten minutes from sign-up to your first reply.

## 1. Create your Workspace

Register an account. The first account creates the Workspace and becomes its
Admin. Invite teammates later from **Settings → Team**.

## 2. Add a Product

Choose **Add Product** in the sidebar (the onboarding wizard does this
for your first Product). Give it a name and the **domains** where the chat
widget will run, for example `www.example.com`.

- `example.com` and `www.example.com` are separate entries.
- `*.example.com` covers subdomains but not the bare `example.com`; add that separately.
- Allow `localhost` explicitly while testing locally.

## 3. Install the widget

Copy the snippet from **Settings → Products → your Product → Widget** and paste
it before `</body>`. See [Install the chat widget](/docs/widget).

## 4. Connect support email

Every Product has a generated inbound address. Forward your support mailbox to
it and replies thread back into the same Conversation. See
[Support email](/docs/email).

## 5. Answer your first message

Send a test chat from your site. It appears in the **Inbox**; reply there and the
visitor sees it live. Continue with [Working the inbox](/docs/agent-workflow).
